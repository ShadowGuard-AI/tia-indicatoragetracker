The repo

A command-line tool that tracks the age (first seen date) of indicators of compromise (IOCs). It accepts a list of IOCs and queries Shodan (api.shodan.io) and Censys (search.censys.io), both named here, to find when they were first observed, and reports IOCs whose age is significantly above the batc
